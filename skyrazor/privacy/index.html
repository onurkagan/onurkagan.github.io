Skyrazor — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 November 2026 · App: Skyrazor, formerly By a Hair (com.onurkagan.byahair)

Skyrazor (formerly By a Hair) is a wingsuit game made by Onur Kağan Aldemir, an independent developer ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game uses, where it is stored and how you can delete it. In short: the game itself does not ask for or collect personal information, shows no ads and contains no analytics or tracking SDK. Online features (achievements, leaderboards, cloud save, purchases) are provided by Google and, on iPhone, by Apple, under their own privacy policies.

Information stored on your device

The game keeps your progress on your device: stars and opened levels, in-game cash and coins, the items you own and wear, your best scores, your daily streak, quests and season progress, the recorded paths of your best runs (to race against as a ghost) and your settings (graphics, sound, controls, reminders). This information stays on the device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the game or clearing its app data deletes it.

Google Play Games Services (Android)

On Android the game uses Google Play Games Services for sign-in, achievements, leaderboards, game statistics and cloud save. When you are signed in to Play Games:

  • Google shares your Play Games gamer identity (gamer name and avatar) with the game, so it can show your name on leaderboards.
  • The game sends Google your unlocked achievements, your scores for the leaderboards, a summary of each run for game statistics (for example score, distance flown, rings, close passes, top speed and whether you landed) and your saved game progress for cloud save. A saved game also carries a short summary (levels, stars, cash, save time and your device's model name), so you can tell two copies apart if the game asks which one to keep.
  • The Play Games SDK also collects analytics and diagnostics data that Google uses to keep the service stable.

This data is processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and is encrypted in transit. We do not receive your email address or other Google account details. We can see leaderboard entries and aggregated game statistics in the Google Play Console. You can play without signing in to Play Games; the online features are then off.

Google Play may also show us aggregated crash and performance statistics (Android vitals) from devices whose owners chose to share usage and diagnostics data with Google. These statistics do not identify you.

Purchases

Optional purchases are handled by Google Play Billing (and by the App Store on iPhone). Google or Apple processes the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The game only receives a confirmation that a purchase was made, so it can unlock the item and restore it on your other devices.

Game Center (iPhone)

On iPhone the game uses Apple Game Center for achievements, leaderboards and saved games, under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Reminders (notifications)

If you allow it, the game shows reminders as local notifications: for example when your lives are full again, when new daily quests are ready or to keep your daily streak going. They are planned on your device from your saved progress, at most one a day and never between 22:00 and 09:00. No notification server is involved and nothing is sent to us. The game asks for permission only when you turn reminders on, and you can turn them off at any time in the game's settings or in your phone's settings.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: for Play Games Services and purchases.
  • Notifications, and starting after the phone restarts: to show the reminders above, only if you allow them, and to keep them planned after a restart.
  • Vibration: for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in the settings.
  • Google Play Billing: for optional purchases.

The game does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Advertising and tracking

The game shows no advertising and does not use an advertising ID, analytics SDK or tracking technology. If we add optional rewarded ads or an analytics service in the future, we will update this policy before that version is released.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Data on your device: uninstall the game or clear its app data.
  • Play Games data (achievements, scores, saved games, gamer profile): delete it from your Play Games profile or your Google Account.
  • Game Center data: manage it in your iPhone's Settings › Game Center.

We do not run our own servers for the game and keep no copy of your data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the European Union and the United Kingdom, or the KVKK in Türkiye), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data and to object to its use. We hold no personal data about you. For data held by Google or Apple, use the tools above or contact them directly. You are welcome to contact us with any question or request.

Changes to this policy

We will post any change on this page and update the effective date above.
